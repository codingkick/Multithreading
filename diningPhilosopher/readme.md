Five philosophers sit at a round table. Each has a plate of food and there is one fork between each pair of philosophers (5 forks total).
A philosopher does only two things — think or eat. To eat, they need both the left and right fork. After eating they put both forks down and go back to thinking.
The problem is — how do you make sure no one starves and there's no deadlock?
The classic deadlock scenario: all 5 philosophers pick up their left fork at the same time → everyone is waiting for the right fork → nobody can eat → deadlock.
